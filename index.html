FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers about the catalog, favorites, themes, and browsing experience.

How are books selected for this catalog?

The catalog focuses on literary classics chosen for historical, cultural, and educational significance across multiple genres.

Do favorites persist after refreshing the page?

Yes. Favorite books are saved in localStorage so they remain available between sessions on the same browser.

Can I use my own cover images?

Yes. Each book entry supports a custom .webp cover path, making it easy to replace placeholder covers with optimized real images.

Is the catalog responsive and accessible?

Yes. The project includes semantic structure, keyboard support, visible focus states, responsive layouts, and accessible modal behavior.
